## 3. The date-of-first-supply — the ONE field that legitimately changes

The original filing said **first supply = 2026-08-01**. That was 2 days in the *future* when you
filed; today it is 10 days in the *past*. **Confirmed 2026-08-10: no EU sale has actually happened**
(non-VIES EU restaurants still can't buy paid plans — that's exactly what this registration unblocks).

**Therefore: enter `2026-10-01`, not `2026-08-01`.**

Typing a past date for a supply that never occurred would be a false statement on a tax registration
— a genuinely worse problem than the field that got you bounced the first time. Copying the original
exactly is the rule *everywhere except here*, and this is why.

**Why 1 October specifically** ([European Commission](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop/register-oss_en)):
registration under the non-Union scheme takes effect **from the first day of the calendar quarter
following the one in which you apply**. Apply in August 2026 with no earlier supply ⇒ effective
**1 October 2026**. You cannot get an earlier date without an actual earlier supply to point at, and
you don't have one. (The backdating route — notify by the 10th of the month after the first supply —
needs a real supply and doesn't apply to you.)

**This costs you nothing in practice.** The code that charges EU VAT at checkout doesn't exist yet;
it gets built once Revenue issues the number. So no EU supply was ever going to happen before
October regardless of what the form says.

Say so plainly in the covering email (§5) — an unexplained date change from August to October is the
kind of thing that generates a query. Explained, it reads as care.

## 6. What unblocks when the EU VAT number lands

Revenue issues a number in the format `EUxxxyyyyyz` plus an effective date. Give Claude both and the
following ships:

- Non-VIES EU restaurants get charged **their own country's VAT** at checkout (Stripe Tax
  `automatic_tax`) instead of hitting the current `euVatSubscriptionBlock` 403.
- Invoices render the real VAT rate/amount row instead of the hardcoded `0.00%`.
- Quarterly OSS return export.
